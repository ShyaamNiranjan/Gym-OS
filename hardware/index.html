Hardware & access control

The door, actually controlled.

Most gym software stops at the register. GymOS goes to the door — a real controller, a real relay, and a real lock, driven by the same credentials your members already carry.

How it works

▣

Step 01

Member presents QR or PIN

At the scanner or keypad mounted by the entrance.

⚙

Step 02

Controller validates instantly

Credential checked live against active membership status.

⎋

Relay unlocks the door

A physical relay drives the lock — no manual buzzer.

Attendance logs automatically

Entry, exit and visit duration recorded without staff input.
